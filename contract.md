**Meeting Logistics**

**Average Work Time Per Week**

Additionally, we are prepared to spend approximately two hours individually working on team deliverables. 

**Communication**
- We will use iMessage and email as our primary forms of communication 
- We plan on having weekly check-ins
- Technologies: We will use Google docs to store our notes, GitHub as version control, and messages as an easy way to share important information from meetings
- We will make unanimous decisions
- Participation: We will divide work each week and we expect everyone to complete their responsibilities
- Procedures for discontinued communication: Communicate with them after class to find out why they have not been communicating and work together to find an effective way to stay in touch


**Conflict Resolution**
- Follow up with the person and remind them to complete their assigned task
- Identify any discrepancies between members' understanding of their tasks and the work assigned to them
- Attempt to resolve the issue in-group
- Consult with Amy if several reminders have been unsuccessful, or if a pattern of problematic behavior persists
